The format

King of the Court

Four courts, 15-minute rounds. You play with and against different people all night, and the games stay close because you start on a court that matches your level.

01

Win, move up. Lose, move down.

Every round ends with movement. Take your court and you climb toward court one; drop a game and you slide down a court. Nobody sits out.

02

Winners bank the points

Only round winners bank points, and we track every player's total all night. Friendly comp, all-level rotations, lots of touches.

03

Top three take the podium

At the end of the night the three highest totals take first, second and third. Then whoever is still standing sorts dinner.

    Questions

    Before you book

    I've never played padel. Can I come?+

    Honestly, not yet. Monday runs matched games at a proper pace, and one player who has never held a racket slows a whole court. Play a few times first, get comfortable serving and rallying, then book. If you want somewhere to start, message us on WhatsApp and we'll point you to a good intro session.

    Do I need my own racket?+

    No. We bring spares. If you have one, bring it. Court shoes or clean trainers are the only thing you need.

    What if I book and can't make it?+

    Tell us on WhatsApp by 2pm on game day. That gives us time to fill your court, and your payment rolls to the next week. After 2pm we can't replace you, one missing player breaks a whole court, so the spot and the payment are burned. No-shows count the same.

    Where are the courts?+

    We rotate between a few courts around Dubai and lock the best one each week. The exact location drops on the group the day before, always evening, always padel-only courts.
