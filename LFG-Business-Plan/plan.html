05  /  The architecture decision

Two ways to
build it.

One question shapes everything: should coaching live on its own separate site and brand, or inside the LFG platform you already have? Both work, and we can build either. Here is how each plays out so you can choose.

Option A

A separate coaching brand

Coaching gets its own name, domain and login, standing apart from the run club.

Advantages

  • Clean, standalone premium positioning, not tied to a free run club.
  • Becomes its own asset you could grow or sell separately later.
  • Freedom to target a different audience beyond the LFG crowd.

Disadvantages

  • You start the audience from zero, the 1,783 do not flow in.
  • A second brand and login for members to learn and trust.
  • Data and CRM split across two systems; slower and costlier to build and run.
Option B

Built inside LFG

Coaching lives in the LFG platform with its own dedicated /coaching space, on one engine.

Advantages

  • The 1,783 members flow straight into coaching, no rebuild.
  • One account, one brand they already know; cross-sell on rails.
  • One list and CRM; faster to launch and cheaper to run.

Disadvantages

  • Coaching shares identity with the free run-club brand.
  • Harder to spin coaching out as a fully separate business later.
  • Premium tier leans on LFG's positioning rather than its own.

Either way it runs on the same engine we build. The choice is positioning: a standalone coaching brand, or coaching powered by the community you already own. We will build whichever you decide.
