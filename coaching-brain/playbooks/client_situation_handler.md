# Playbook: Client Situation Handler

**Task:** The coach describes a situation ("she's gone quiet", "he's frustrated with the scale", "wants to quit"). The brain gives the read and the message to send, in Ahmed's voice.

**Load:** `knowledge/behavior-coaching/04_client_situations_playbook.md`, `02_motivational_interviewing.md`, `05_communication_and_difficult_conversations.md` + `ahmed-method/06_voice_and_communication.md`, `07_client_handling_and_retention.md`.

## Common situations → the move
- **Gone quiet / ghosting** → low-pressure, warm re-engagement; reference their goal, not their absence. Never guilt-trip.
- **Missed sessions / slipping adherence** → normalize the lapse, shrink the next step (Fogg tiny-habit), rebuild momentum. Curiosity, not judgment.
- **Frustrated with the scale / plateau** → widen the lens to non-scale wins; explain trend vs reading; check the real constraint before changing the plan.
- **Demotivated** → reconnect to the "why" (MI evoking); a quick win this week; autonomy (let them choose the next step).
- **Wants to quit** → don't sell; ask open questions, understand the real reason (cost, results, life, fit); reflect it back; offer a genuine path or a graceful exit.
- **Complaint / unhappy** → acknowledge fully first, no defensiveness; own what's ours; propose a concrete fix.
- **Life event (illness, travel, stress, injury)** → shift to maintenance/damage-control mode; protect the relationship over the program; make coming back easy.
- **Body-image sensitivity** → careful, affirming language; focus on function and health; never comment on appearance in a loaded way.

## Output format
```
READ: [what's likely really going on]
MOVE: [the approach]
MESSAGE (Ahmed's voice): [ready-to-send draft]
IF THEY [react X]: [next line]
BOUNDARY/REFERRAL: [if applicable — when to hold a line or refer out]
```

## Rules
- **Acknowledge before advising.** Every hard situation starts with the client feeling heard.
- Match Ahmed's tone: warm, direct, human, encouraging; his emoji/formatting habits (see voice doc).
- Clinical territory (disordered eating, mental health, injury) → support the person and **refer to a professional**; the brain does not treat.
