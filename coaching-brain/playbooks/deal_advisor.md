# Playbook: Deal / Pricing Advisor

**Task:** Help Ahmed decide what to offer a lead or existing client and how to frame it, based on LFG's real approach and premium positioning.

**Load:** `ahmed-method/08_sales_and_deals.md`, `02_discovery_and_consultation.md` + `knowledge/behavior-coaching/03_adherence_and_retention.md` (for renewals).

## Inputs
Who is this (new lead / trial / existing client / renewal) · their goal and urgency · how warm they are (run-club member, referral, cold) · budget signals · what they've bought before · which tier fits (challenge / small-group / 1:1).

## Method
1. **Fit before price.** Recommend the tier that actually serves their goal, not the most expensive. The right fit retains; a mis-sold high tier churns.
2. **Anchor on value and outcome**, not hours. Frame the 12-week transformation and what's included, then the price (see the offer stack in the LFG plan).
3. **Use real, ethical scarcity** (genuine spot limits, dated cohorts). Never fake urgency.
4. **Match the LFG ladder**: free run → membership → challenge → small-group → premium 1:1. Move people one rung at a time.
5. **Renewals**: start the renewal conversation from results achieved and the next goal, before the program ends, not after.
6. **Objections**: surface the real objection (price is usually a proxy for uncertainty about results) and address that with proof and the guarantee.

## Output format
```
RECOMMENDED OFFER: [tier + framing]
WHY THIS FITS THEM: [1-2 lines]
HOW TO PRESENT IT (Ahmed's voice): [talking points or message]
OBJECTION PREP: [likely objection → response]
FALLBACK: [a lower rung if they hesitate — keep them in the ecosystem]
```

## Rules
- Never invent specific prices that aren't set by LFG; if a price isn't known, ask Ahmed or present the structure and let him fill the number.
- Premium positioning: sell the outcome and the system, discount rarely and only with a reason (founding cohort, referral), never erode the anchor.
- Keep it honest — no fabricated proof, no pressure tactics that would damage the LFG community's trust.
