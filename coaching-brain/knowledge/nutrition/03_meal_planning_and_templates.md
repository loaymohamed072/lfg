# 03 — Meal Planning & Templates (LFG Coaching Brain)

> **Sources synthesized:** Precision Nutrition (hand-portion system), Eric Helms *Muscle & Strength Pyramid* (meal frequency & distribution), ISSN Position Stand: Nutrient Timing (Kerksick et al.) & Aragon/Schoenfeld peri-workout meta-analysis, Renaissance Periodization (meal structuring around training), registered-dietitian consensus (food lists, eating-out strategy). Original explanations; halal/regional food choices added for the LFG (Dubai) client base.

This file turns the numbers from file 02 into **food on a plate the client will actually eat.** Core belief: we don't hand clients a rigid 5-meal prescription. We give them a **flexible template + food lists + portion tools** so they can hit targets with foods they like, within their culture, budget, and schedule.

---

## 1. Build the plan AROUND the client (intake before templates)

Before choosing any template, the copilot collects and respects:

- **Preferences & dislikes** — never program foods the client hates; adherence dies instantly.
- **Allergies / intolerances** — hard constraints (dairy, gluten, nuts, shellfish, eggs). Swap, don't argue.
- **Culture / religion** — **halal** by default for the LFG base; no pork, no alcohol as a "budget" item; include regional foods (see §5).
- **Medical/GI** — IBS, reflux, etc. → route to the special-cases file *before* building; do not default to high-fiber.
- **Cooking skill, time, budget** — a client with 10 min and no cooking skill gets assembly meals and convenience protein, not a recipe list.
- **Schedule & training time** — anchors where the biggest carb + protein meals go (§6).
- **Meal count preference** — 3 vs 4 vs 5 meals is a *comfort* choice; total daily intake matters more than frequency.

**Rule:** the template is a scaffold. The client's real life decides the foods that fill it.

## 2. Two ways to hit targets — pick per client

### A) Tracking (weighing/logging, e.g. MacroFactor / MyFitnessPal)
Most precise. Best for clients who like data, want faster results, or are lean/advanced. Track protein + calories tightly, fat/carb loosely.

### B) Hand-portion method (Precision Nutrition) — no scale, no app
Best for beginners, the app-averse, busy clients, and eating out. The client's **own hand** scales to their body:

| Food group | 1 portion = | Women/meal | Men/meal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Protein** | 1 **palm** (thickness + size of palm) | 1 palm | 2 palms |
| **Vegetables** | 1 **fist** | 1 fist | 2 fists |
| **Carbs** (grains, starches, fruit) | 1 **cupped hand** | 1 cupped hand | 2 cupped hands |
| **Fats** (oils, nuts, butter, cheese) | 1 **thumb** | 1 thumb | 2 thumbs |

Base plate: **3–4 meals/day** of the above. Adjust portions up for muscle gain, down (usually carbs/fats) for faster fat loss. Rough energy anchors: 1 palm protein ≈ 20–30 g protein; 1 cupped hand carbs ≈ 20–25 g carbs; 1 thumb fat ≈ 8–12 g fat.

### The universal plate (works for either method)
> **½ plate non-starchy veg · ¼ plate lean protein (a palm) · ¼ plate smart carbs · a thumb of fat · water.**
Add fruit and dairy to taste within the budget. This one image solves most meals without any math.

## 3. High-protein food lists (halal-friendly, by source)

**Animal (highest quality, complete):**
- Chicken breast/thigh, turkey, lean beef, veal, lamb (halal) — ~25–31 g protein / 100 g
- Fish: cod, hammour, tuna, salmon, tilapia, prawns/shrimp — ~20–26 g / 100 g
- Eggs (~6 g each) & egg whites (~3.5 g each / ~11 g per 100 ml carton)
- Greek yogurt / labneh (~10 g / 100 g), skyr, cottage cheese (~11 g / 100 g), quark
- Milk (~3.4 g / 100 ml), low-fat cheese

**Plant (great for vegetarian/vegan, combine sources for completeness):**
- Lentils, chickpeas (hummus base), black beans, kidney beans (~8–9 g / 100 g cooked)
- Tofu (~8 g / 100 g), tempeh (~19 g / 100 g), edamame (~11 g / 100 g)
- Soy milk (~3.3 g / 100 ml), seitan (high but not for gluten-sensitive)
- Quinoa (~4 g / 100 g cooked, complete), nuts/seeds (protein + fat — budget the fat)

**Convenience / high-leverage protein (for busy or low-appetite clients):**
- Whey or vegan protein powder (~20–25 g/scoop), protein yogurts, ready-cooked chicken, tinned tuna/salmon, protein bars (watch calories), edamame packs, boiled eggs.

**Copilot heuristic:** if a client is missing protein target daily, the fix is almost always *a scoop of whey, a tub of Greek yogurt, or an extra palm of meat/fish at 2 meals* — not a redesign.

## 4. Sample day templates at different calorie levels

All templates: **hit protein first, then fill with the client's preferred carbs/fats/veg.** Foods are examples — swap freely.

### ~1500 kcal (P150 / C130 / F45) — smaller female, fat loss
- **Breakfast:** 200 g Greek yogurt + 1 scoop whey + berries + ½ cup oats. (~P45 C40 F8)
- **Lunch:** 130 g chicken breast + big salad + 100 g cooked rice + 1 tsp olive oil. (~P40 C40 F8)
- **Snack:** 1 apple + 30 g almonds *or* protein shake. (~P25 C20 F12)
- **Dinner:** 150 g white fish (hammour) + fist of veg + 150 g potato. (~P40 C30 F5)

### ~1800 kcal (P140 / C185 / F55) — the worked-example client (file 02)
- **Breakfast:** 3 eggs + 2 egg whites + 1 slice toast + tomato. (~P30 C20 F18)
- **Lunch:** 150 g chicken + 180 g cooked rice + veg + 1 tbsp olive oil. (~P45 C55 F15)
- **Pre/post training:** 200 g Greek yogurt + banana + honey. (~P22 C40 F5)
- **Dinner:** 150 g lean beef/lamb + fist of veg + 200 g potato. (~P43 C40 F17)

### ~2200 kcal (P175 / C230 / F65) — active male, maintenance / recomp
- **Breakfast:** 80 g oats + 1 scoop whey + banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter. (~P40 C70 F14)
- **Lunch:** 200 g chicken + 250 g cooked rice + veg + olive oil. (~P55 C80 F16)
- **Snack:** cottage cheese 200 g + fruit. (~P25 C25 F5)
- **Dinner:** 200 g salmon + fist veg + 250 g potato. (~P50 C55 F25)

### ~2600 kcal (P190 / C300 / F70) — muscle gain
- Scale the above up: add a 4th–5th meal, more rice/potato/oats/fruit around training, keep protein ~1 g/lb, don't let fat balloon.

## 5. Regional / Dubai food swaps (hit macros without losing the culture)

| Craving / dish | Coach-it version |
|---|---|
| Shawarma | Chicken shawarma, extra meat, skip the extra oil/mayo, half the bread or laffa-in-a-bowl over salad |
| Rice dishes (biryani, kabsa, machboos) | Load the meat/protein, moderate the rice portion (1 cupped hand), watch added ghee/oil |
| Hummus / labneh / moutabal | Great — protein + fiber; portion the olive-oil drizzle (it's the calorie driver) |
| Grilled meats / mixed grill | Excellent default — lean protein; watch the buttered rice and bread |
| Manakish / bread | Za'atar/labneh over cheese versions; count the bread as the carb portion |
| Dates | Fine pre-training carb; 2–3 dates ≈ a cupped hand of carbs (big in Ramadan, §6 special-cases) |
| Karak / fancy coffees | These are **liquid calories** — order less sugar / smaller / skip. Common hidden 150–300 kcal. |

## 6. Meal timing around training (the low-priority-but-easy-win layer)

Timing is level 4 of the hierarchy — **useful at the margins, never the main event.** The "anabolic window" is wide (several hours), not 30 minutes (Aragon/Schoenfeld). Get the day's totals right first. Then, for a bit of extra performance/recovery:

- **Pre-training (1–3 h before):** a meal with protein (20–40 g) + carbs to fuel the session. If training fasted/early, a small carb + protein hit (banana + whey, or dates) helps output.
- **Post-training (within ~2 h):** protein (~0.4 g/kg, 20–40 g) + carbs to refuel glycogen and support recovery. No need to sprint to a shaker.
- **Carb placement:** bias the day's carbs toward the meals **around training** — best performance and glycogen use, and it often improves training-day satiety and sleep.
- **Protein distribution:** ~3–5 feedings of 20–40 g across the day (ISSN) tops up muscle-protein-synthesis. A refinement, not a requirement.
- **Before bed:** a slow protein (casein, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, ~30–40 g) is a fine option, especially for gaining clients — optional, not mandatory.

## 7. Snack & eating-out strategies (adherence in the real world)

**Smart snacks (protein-forward, portion-controlled):**
Greek yogurt/labneh + fruit · protein shake · boiled eggs · edamame · cottage cheese · jerky/biltong · a piece of fruit + a thumb of nuts · hummus + veg sticks · protein bar (watch kcal).

**Eating out — the 4 rules the copilot gives every client:**
1. **Anchor a lean protein** (grilled chicken/fish/meat, kebab, shawarma-in-a-bowl).
2. **Fill on veg/salad** for volume and fiber; dressing on the side.
3. **Pick ONE indulgence** — the bread, *or* the dessert, *or* the fries, not all three.
4. **Watch liquid calories** — juice, soda, karak, cocktails, mocktails; water/diet drinks/black coffee are free.
Extra: use hand portions when there's no scale; eat slowly (fullness lags ~20 min); it's one meal, not the diet — the weekly average is what matters.

**Alcohol note (where relevant):** 7 kcal/g, lowers inhibition (→ overeating) and blunts recovery; if consumed, budget it and keep protein/adherence intact around it. For the halal LFG base this is usually a non-issue.

## 8. Copilot meal-planning checklist

1. Pull targets from file 02 (kcal, P/F/C, fiber, protein-per-meal).
2. Collect preferences, allergies, culture, GI issues, schedule, cooking capacity.
3. Choose **tracking vs hand-portion**.
4. Lock **protein sources first** at each meal (palm/scoop/tub).
5. Fill carbs around training, fats to the floor, veg for volume/fiber.
6. Offer **swaps and regional options** so nothing feels forbidden.
7. Add **2–3 default snacks** and the **eating-out rules**.
8. Keep it to the fewest rules that work → maximize adherence.
