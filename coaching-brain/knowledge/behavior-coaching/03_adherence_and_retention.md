# Adherence & Retention

> Sources: exercise-adherence research (self-monitoring/action-planning as strongest adherence levers; ~50%+ dropout within 6 months; median dropout ~14 weeks); fitness-app & studio retention benchmarks 2025–26 (industry ~66% annual gym retention; fitness apps ~9.2% monthly churn; ~38% of cancellations cite lost motivation; first-90-days and first-2-weeks as highest-risk windows; 3+ sessions/week → 85–90% retention); ICF coaching retention (~50–65%); SDT & habit science (files 01–02). Benchmarks are directional, drawn from public 2025–26 industry reports, not LFG's own data. Original explanations.

---

## 0. How the copilot should use this file

Retention is the business. For a premium 12-week coaching program, a client who quits at week 4 is a refund risk, a lost renewal, a lost referral, and a bad testimonial. **Adherence (does she do the work?) drives results, and results + relationship drive retention (does she stay and renew?).** This file gives the copilot the levers to protect both, plus the numbers to benchmark against and the scripts for the moments that decide whether a client stays.

**LFG frame:** hybrid (app + WhatsApp + in-person run club/bootcamp), premium price, 12-week cycles, largely-female GCC professionals. The in-person community is a retention *superpower* most online coaches don't have — the copilot should push the coach to route online clients into it.

## 1. Why clients drop off (rank-ordered, with the fix)

From adherence research and churn data, the dominant reasons — and what each actually signals:

| Reason for dropout | What it really is | Primary fix (file ref) |
|---|---|---|
| **Lost motivation / goal abandoned** (~top cause, ~38% of app cancels) | Motivation was the plan's fuel; it ran out | Build habits & systems so adherence doesn't need motivation (file 01 §3); MI to reconnect the "why" (file 02) |
| **No early results / progress felt invisible** | Competence need starved (SDT); watching only the scale | Non-scale wins & performance tracking (file 07); set expectations early |
| **Time conflict / life got busy** (the #1 *stated* reason) | Ability barrier, not a values problem | Shrink sessions (10-min minimum), flexible scheduling, remove friction (file 01 §6) |
| **Plan too hard / too complex** | Ability + competence frustrated | Simplify; one habit at a time (PN method); scale difficulty to skill |
| **Felt like a number / weak relationship** | Relatedness starved | High-touch check-ins, community, personal WhatsApp presence |
| **Unrealistic expectations unmet** | Mismatch set at onboarding | Fix at intake; honest timelines (file 04, file 06) |
| **Cost / value doubt** (premium-specific) | Client can't see what she's paying for | Make progress visible; articulate the value; renewal framing (§7) |

**The two danger windows the copilot must flag:**
- **First 14 days** — the single most predictive churn signal. Clients who complete fewer than ~3 sessions in the first two weeks churn at several times the rate of those who build an early weekly habit. *Front-load attention here.*
- **First 90 days** — roughly half of members who ever quit do so in the first 90 days. A 12-week program *is* this window end-to-end, so the entire program is the high-risk zone. There is no coasting period.

> **Decision rule:** If a new client hasn't completed 3 sessions by day 14, escalate immediately — personal message, remove a friction point, shrink the plan. This is the highest-ROI intervention in the whole system.

## 2. Retention benchmarks (what "good" looks like)

Use these as directional targets, not gospel — LFG should track its own numbers and beat these.

- **Gym/studio annual retention:** industry average ~66% (boutique studios ~55–65% retention / 35–45% churn; big-box worse).
- **Coaching client retention (ICF):** typically ~50–65%, varies by model.
- **Fitness apps:** ~9.2% monthly churn (~68% annual) — app-only is leaky; high-touch coaching should beat it substantially.
- **The frequency effect:** members training **3+×/week retain at ~85–90%**; 2×/week drops to ~65–75%. Session frequency is the strongest retention lever you can directly coach.

**LFG-relevant targets to aim past:**
- **12-week completion rate** (finished vs. dropped): aim >80% — this is the number premium coaching lives or dies on.
- **Renewal / rebuy rate** at end of 12 weeks: this is the real profitability metric (below).
- **Referral rate:** community + results should make this a channel, not an accident.

> The copilot's north star: get clients to **3+ sessions/week within the first two weeks**, and keep them there. Almost every retention benefit flows from that single behavior.

## 3. Onboarding for retention — the first 14 days decide everything

Because early inactivity predicts churn, onboarding isn't admin — it's the highest-leverage retention work. A strong LFG onboarding:

1. **First-session-fast.** Get her to complete one real workout within 48 hours of signing up. Early wins beat perfect plans. (Ability lever: make session one short and winnable.)
2. **Set honest expectations** (kills the #1 quiet killer — unmet expectations): "Fat loss shows on the scale in weeks 3–4, not week 1. You'll *feel* stronger and sleep better first. The scale bounces daily — we track the trend." (file 07)
3. **One or two starting habits, not ten** (PN: one habit at a time; confirm ≥9/10 confidence). Overwhelm at onboarding is a top dropout cause.
4. **Set up the environment & prompts** — app notifications, calendar blocks, habit stacks, WhatsApp check-in cadence agreed (file 01 §6).
5. **Immediate relatedness** — a personal welcome (voice note, not a template), invite to the run club/bootcamp *in week one*, introduce her to the community. Turn an online client into a community member fast.
6. **Define the scoreboard together** — the process goals she'll be measured on (autonomy + competence), and how you'll track non-scale progress (file 07).

> **Copilot onboarding checklist trigger:** New client signed → (1) welcome voice note today, (2) session 1 booked within 48h, (3) 1–2 habits set at ≥9/10 confidence, (4) run-club/bootcamp invite sent, (5) expectations + tracking method explained, (6) day-14 review flagged.

## 4. Check-in cadence & structure

The rhythm that keeps clients accountable and cared for without burning out the coach. Self-monitoring and action-planning are the *most evidence-backed adherence levers* — the check-in is where they happen.

**Recommended hybrid cadence (premium, 12-week):**
- **Async daily (light):** in-app habit/workout logging; a WhatsApp thread that's alive (not a broadcast channel). Silence from a client for several days is a churn signal — the copilot should flag it.
- **Weekly check-in (structured):** the backbone. A consistent format the client submits (weight trend, measurements/photos-or-alternatives, training completed, energy/sleep/hunger, wins, obstacles) + a coach response within ~24h.
- **In-person touch:** run club / bootcamp attendance as the relatedness anchor.
- **Milestone reviews:** week-4, week-8, week-12 deeper reviews — assess progress, adjust plan, and (at week 8–10) start the renewal conversation.

**A repeatable weekly check-in response structure (coach it to the coach):**
1. **Affirm** a specific win first (relatedness + competence). "Three sessions in a heavy work week — that's the habit locking in."
2. **Read the data as a trend**, not a snapshot (file 07). Reassure on normal weight fluctuation.
3. **One adjustment, not five.** Change one variable so cause/effect stays clear and she isn't overwhelmed.
4. **One question** (MI-style) to surface any hidden obstacle.
5. **Confirm this week's process goals** + confidence check.

> **Decision rule:** Never send a check-in that's all correction and no affirmation. Ratio should lean positive. Clients quit coaches who make them feel like they're failing.

## 5. Celebrating wins (the satisfaction law, operationalized)

Immediate, visible reward is what makes a habit stick (Atomic Habits Law 4) and what feeds competence (SDT). Coaches under-do this. The copilot should proactively surface wins the coach can celebrate:

- **Non-scale wins:** first unbroken 5k, +5kg on a lift, jeans fitting, better sleep, energy, a HYROX station PR, a full week of habits hit.
- **Streaks & consistency:** "you've trained 3×/week for four straight weeks" is often more motivating than a weight number.
- **Make it public (with consent):** community shout-outs in the run club/bootcamp, a client win channel — social recognition is powerful *and* privacy-sensitive, so **always get consent** and offer opt-out (especially for female clients uneasy about visibility).
- **Milestone rituals:** a message/badge at week 4, 8, 12; a small reward at program completion.

## 6. Re-engaging lapsing & at-risk clients

**Detect early (the copilot watches for these signals):**
- No workouts logged in 5–7 days.
- Missed a scheduled check-in.
- Tone shift in messages (short, flat, "fine").
- Stopped attending run club/bootcamp.
- <3 sessions in first 14 days (new client).

**Re-engagement protocol (warm, low-pressure, MI-based — file 02):**
1. **Reach out personally and fast** — a voice note beats a text; name the specific gap without blame: "Noticed this week's been quiet — no judgment, just checking in on you. How are you doing?"
2. **Diagnose the lever** (file 01 §1): prompt, ability, or motivation? Ask, don't assume.
3. **Shrink the re-entry** — offer a tiny win, not the full plan back (lapse protocol, file 01 §8). "Let's just get one session in this week — that's it."
4. **Reconnect the why** (MI evoking questions) if motivation faded.
5. **Fix the actual barrier** — reschedule, simplify, adjust for life stress (file 04).
6. **Never shame.** Shame accelerates the exit.

> **Copilot next-best-move for a ghosting client:** personal voice note → 1 diagnostic question → offer a 1-session re-entry + a friction removed → invite to next run club as a low-stakes reconnect. Escalate to a call if no response in 3–4 days.

## 7. Renewal conversations

The renewal (rebuy at end of the 12 weeks) is where premium coaching becomes profitable — acquiring a new client costs far more than retaining one. Renewal isn't a sales pitch bolted on at week 12; it's earned across the program and *teed up early*.

**Principles:**
- **Start early:** plant it at week 8–10, not the final session. "As we head into the last few weeks, let's talk about where you want to be next."
- **Anchor on progress + the next goal** (not on "do you want to continue?"). Show the transformation (the tracked data and non-scale wins are the receipts — file 07), then point at the next milestone: "You went from can't-run-2k to a 5k. The half-marathon / first HYROX / next strength block is the obvious next chapter."
- **Use SDT:** the next block should feel *autonomous* (her goal, her choice), *competence-building* (a clear new challenge), and keep the *relatedness* she's built with you and the community.
- **Make the value visible.** Premium clients renew when they can *see* what they got. If progress felt invisible, that's a tracking failure, not a pricing problem.
- **Handle the "I'll do it on my own now" objection** with MI, not pressure: explore it, affirm autonomy, and honestly frame what changes without support (usually: adherence drops without accountability — the very thing coaching provides).

**Renewal conversation skeleton:**
1. Recap the wins together (show the data). 
2. Ask what she's proud of / what changed for her. (Her own change talk.)
3. Ask what she wants next. (Evoke the next goal.)
4. Frame the next block around that goal.
5. Make the offer clearly, then respect her autonomy.

## 8. Retention metrics for the coaching business (what to track)

The copilot should help the coach watch these, not just individual clients:

- **Session frequency per client per week** (leading indicator — 3+ is the target).
- **14-day activation rate** (% of new clients completing 3+ sessions in first 2 weeks).
- **12-week completion rate** (% finishing vs. dropping).
- **Renewal / rebuy rate** at program end.
- **Churn rate** (monthly and per-cohort) — benchmark against ~9% app churn / ~66% annual gym retention; premium high-touch should beat both.
- **Check-in response/submission rate** (engagement proxy).
- **Referral rate** (community + results health-check).
- **Client-reported satisfaction / NPS** at week 6 and 12.

> **The one-line retention thesis for the copilot:** *Get her active in the first 14 days, keep her at 3+ sessions/week, make progress visible (beyond the scale), keep the relationship warm and the community close, catch lapses fast, and tee up the next goal before week 12 ends.* Everything else is detail.
