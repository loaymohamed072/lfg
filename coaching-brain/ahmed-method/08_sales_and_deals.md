# 08 — Sales, Pricing, Packages & Deals

> Source: 29 real LFG WhatsApp coaching threads, Sept 2023 → June 2026. All examples de-identified. **Discipline rule:** this file reports only what actually appears in the threads. Anything not directly evidenced is labelled **(INFERRED)** and must be treated as a hypothesis, not fact. No prices are invented; only the one explicit price found in the data is stated.

---

## 0. What the data does and doesn't contain

These are coaching-delivery chats, not sales-call transcripts. The hard sell / discovery call mostly happens **before** these threads open (many threads start with "Thanks for the call earlier and super excited to start"). So this file captures the sales signals that leak into the coaching relationship: onboarding-as-close, renewals, referral incentives, discount codes, and one explicit special-rate quote. Treat gaps honestly.

---

## 1. Business & program model (evidenced)

- **Brand:** LFG (LFG Dubai). Dubai/UAE-based. Coaches: Ahmed Sam (head), Coach Omar / "LFG Dubai."
- **Delivery:** online + in-person hybrid. There are **bootcamps**, **PT (personal training) sessions/classes**, a **coaching app** (check-ins, workout plans, food logs), and **video/Loom check-in reviews**.
- **Program length:** cycles of **12 weeks / 3 months** are the default unit ("your strength goals for this next 12 weeks," "3 month coaching"). Longer engagements exist (references to 4-month, 8-week, and "9 month journey" arrangements).
- **Cohort intake:** clients are onboarded in **cohorts** — the data references **"LFG14"** and **"LFG14 cohort"** as an intake batch. One client was "onboarded from the LFG14 cohort, 4 months instead of 3."
- **Capacity is real and named:** "yess we are full 160 people right now, trying to see if anyone cancels." Scarcity is genuine, not manufactured.
- **Tools/stack seen:** onboarding email + app onboarding form; **Kahunas** (coaching platform — a `kahunas.io/coach/payment/link/...` payment link appears); **meetntrain.com** (class/PT credit packages — a `meetntrain.com/package/12-credits-...` link appears); Dr. Nutrition / UFC Gym for BMI/body analysis tests.

## 2. Pricing (only what is explicitly in the data)

- **One explicit price appears:** a bespoke **special rate of AED 2,000 for two months (an 8-week lock-in)**, quoted directly to an existing client who requested a special rate:
  > "As discussed we'll do an 8 week lock in... regarding the special rate as requested it will just be **AED 2,000 for the two months**. Let me know if u can do bank transfer, payment link or cash, im fine with either way. Thanks bro 🙏🏼"
- **This is the ONLY hard number in the corpus.** It was framed as a special/requested rate for a returning client, so it is **not** the standard list price. Do NOT quote it as LFG's standard pricing.
- **Package/credit products exist** (referenced, price not shown in-thread): a "12 credits" class/PT package via meetntrain.com; a standing coaching subscription; a "Practical meal plan" subscription (Ahmed once asked the group for "your renewal dates" for it).
- **Payment methods offered (evidenced):** bank transfer, payment link (Kahunas), or cash — "im fine with either way." Flexible and low-friction.

**Copilot rule:** never state a price the user hasn't given you. If asked "what does it cost," surface only that packages/subscriptions exist, payment is flexible (transfer / link / cash), and defer the actual number to the coach/current rate card.

## 3. Onboarding IS the close (evidenced)

The moment a deal is agreed, Ahmed's onboarding sequence does the "welcome + lock-in" work:
1. Hyped welcome shouting the name: "Hi [NAME]!! Super excited to start your transformation 😍😍😍🔥."
2. Sets the container: "This group is among you, Coach Ahmed and Coach Omar — we'll keep all communication here to give u full support."
3. Immediate next steps to create momentum and commitment: onboarding form, food pics, BMI/body test, measurements, 12-week goals, "you should've received an onboarding email — lmk if u got it."
4. Co-coach reinforces: "Pleasure having you onboard 💪🏽 cant wait to work together!!"

This front-loaded excitement + clear next steps is a retention/activation device: it gets the client acting (and invested) within the first hour.

## 4. Renewals (evidenced)

- Renewals are **expected and normalized**, handled inside the ongoing relationship, not re-pitched cold.
- Renewal admin is light and warm: "if you can send a screenshot after you're done with the renewal that'd be great 🙌🏽"; "you can do the renewal next month."
- Cohort length can shift the renewal date and Ahmed/Omar flex on it: "you were onboarded from LFG14, 4 months instead of 3 🙌🏽 we good, you can do the renewal next month."
- Ahmed periodically audits subscription renewal dates at the group level: "whoever is subscribed to Practical meal plan please update me on your renewal dates."
- **The renewal engine is the check-in recap.** Right before renewal moments, the LFG side sends a milestone recap making progress undeniable ("✅ What went well last 3 months: hip thrusts 20kg → 60kg 😮‍💨🔥, noticeable muscle changes, improved consistency..."). Progress made visible = renewal feels obvious. **(INFERRED that this is deliberately timed to renewal, but the recap → renewal adjacency is present in the data.)**

**Renewal template (Ahmed voice):**
> [name] look at what we did these last 3 months 🔥 [2–3 specific wins + numbers]. Proud of u wallah 💪🏽 this is momentum we dont wanna lose. Lets keep building into the next block — whenever u do the renewal just send me a screenshot 🙌🏽

## 5. Referral engine (evidenced — this is LFG's strongest visible growth lever)

Ahmed repeatedly offers **"a free month of coaching" when an existing client refers someone who signs up.** This appears multiple times:
- "If they sign up with us u get a month free!!"
- "If he signs up will give u a month free coaching"
- "im just saying if he ends up in the program u getting a thank you month free"
- "Hopefully if she joins (she said she will) u get an extra month on the house."

Pattern: casual, generous, framed as a "thank you," offered in the flow of normal chat — not a formal program. Very effective because it rides on the warm relationship.

**Referral template:**
> Btw if [prospect] ends up joining us, u get a thank-you month free on the house 🙌🏽 no pressure at all, just know the offer's there 🔥

## 6. Discounts & promotions (evidenced)

- **Seasonal promos exist and are used as urgency:** an **EID discount on packages** — "Did u avail our EID discount on the packages?" and "we extended it till this Sunday so get it before it goes." Classic deadline urgency, lightly applied.
- **Discount codes are distributed** to clients/cohorts: "@LFG Dubai will send them their discount codes."
- **Partner/affiliate discount codes** for supplements/products are shared as a value-add (e.g. a hydration-product code "LFG20," a supplement discount) — "I asked them for a discount code 🤣." Builds goodwill, not core revenue.

**Copilot rule:** urgency is applied gently and truthfully (real deadlines, real capacity), never fabricated scarcity.

## 7. Upsells / cross-sells (evidenced, light)

- **Additional packages** offered to existing clients for future use: "Do u want to get a package for urself u can use when u come?" (offering a class-credit package to a client who travels in and out).
- **Add-on services** referenced: bootcamps, PT sessions, meal-plan subscriptions — clients can layer these on the base coaching.
- **In-package clarifications** to reduce friction and reassure value: "Jenny it is included in ur package!" (tells a client something is already covered rather than upselling it — builds trust).

## 8. Objection handling & closing style (evidenced + inferred)

**Evidenced patterns:**
- **Special-rate flexibility:** when a client asked for a special rate, Ahmed accommodated with a clear bespoke quote (AED 2,000 / 2 months) and flexible payment. He negotiates, he doesn't rigidly gatekeep price.
- **Ties the offer to a concrete, time-bound goal to justify the commitment:** "8 week lock in... u have [event] at mid august, that gives us enough time to lean out in 8 weeks if we really lock in. I wanna be able to see ur abs bro and thats smth u always wanted and i feel we can do it." → he sells the *outcome and the belief*, then attaches the price almost as an afterthought.
- **Low-pressure, relationship-first tone on every money touchpoint.** Money asks are short, warm, and end with "🙏🏼 thanks bro." No hard-closing language.
- **Scarcity is honest** ("we're full 160 people right now").

**(INFERRED)** The primary close likely happens on a **discovery/onboarding call** before these chats begin (threads repeatedly open with "thanks for the call earlier"). The WhatsApp layer is for warm continuation, renewals, referrals, and bespoke rate requests — not cold closing.

**Closing template (goal-anchored, Ahmed voice):**
> Good catching up bro 🙌🏼 so as discussed lets do an [X week] lock-in — u've got [their real constraint/event], which gives us enough time to [specific outcome they want] if we really lock in. That's smth u've always wanted and I genuinely feel we can do it. [Rate + payment: transfer / link / cash, whatever's easier for u]. Thanks bro 🙏🏼

## 9. The LFG sales philosophy in one line

Sell the transformation and the belief, keep the relationship warm the whole way through, let the client's own progress do the renewing, and grow through generous referrals ("month free") rather than pressure. Price is flexible and quiet; the outcome and the relationship are loud.

---

## 10. Hard constraints for the copilot on anything sales-related

1. **Never invent or state a price.** The only real number in the data (AED 2,000 / 2 months) was a special one-off rate — do not present it as standard. Defer pricing to the coach / current rate card.
2. **Referral = "month free"** is the go-to incentive; offer it generously and casually.
3. **Renewals ride on progress recaps,** not cold re-pitches. Make wins visible first.
4. **Urgency only when real** (genuine deadlines like EID promo, genuine capacity limits).
5. **Anchor every offer to the client's own stated goal and a timeframe**, then keep the money ask short, warm, and flexible on payment.
6. Flag every non-evidenced claim as INFERRED. Do not fabricate package tiers, prices, or terms that aren't in the source.
