## Credentialed models & frameworks he was trained on

These are the formal tools from the coursework that surface in how Ahmed coaches every client, not only pre/postnatal ones.

### 1. Screening → risk → referral pathway
The backbone of his intake discipline:
- **Pre-activity screening via PAR-Q** before programming anyone.
- **Contra-indication logic:** distinguish **absolute** vs **relative** contra-indications.
  - *Absolute* → do **not** train (medical clearance required first).
  - *Relative* → **GP referral / clearance** before proceeding.
- **Informed consent** — a signed agreement between trainer and client outlining benefits and risks.
- Knows which gathered information *mandates* referral to a GP or health professional.

### 2. FITT programming principle
Every session designed on **F**requency, **I**ntensity, **T**ime, **T**ype, with a structured **warm-up → main component → cool-down (incl. post-workout stretch)** and stage-appropriate exercise selection, adaptations, and intensity checks.

### 3. Intensity monitoring by RPE
Uses **Rate of Perceived Exertion** to regulate load, scaled down as demand/risk rises. In the assessed pre/postnatal work the RPE targets stepped down across stages (roughly 7–8 → 6–7 → 4–6 across trimesters, ~4–5 postnatal) — evidence he programs intensity to physiological state, not ego.

### 4. Formal risk assessment (4-step)
For any flagged exercise: **(1)** identify the hazard → **(2)** rate likelihood of harm (High/Medium/Low) → **(3)** rate level of harm to client → **(4)** state the control measure. Applied per stage/context.

### 5. Warning-sign / stop-exercise protocol
Trained to recognise red flags (e.g. vaginal bleeding, leaking fluid, calf pain/swelling) and to respond professionally and sensitively: **stop exercise and obtain doctor approval before continuing.**

### 6. Credible-source discipline
Defers to recognised authorities rather than opinion: **NHS, ACOG, RCOG** for pre/postnatal guidance. Trained to communicate benefits and refer to these bodies when a client raises fear-based concerns.

### 7. Legal & ethical responsibility + rapport
Coursework covered legal/ethical duties of care and building an effective working relationship through **active listening, trust, and mutual respect** — the consultative, client-first tone that shows up in his frameworks.

## Evidence-based nutrition knowledge (his authored handout)

Ahmed personally authored the award's A4 nutrition handout ("Evidence based guidance by Ahmed"). It demonstrates the applied nutrition science behind his client work:

- **Energy needs across pregnancy** (context-specific caloric adjustment):
  - Trimesters 1–2: generally **no extra calories** — prioritise food *quality* over quantity; small frequent meals for nausea/energy.
  - Trimester 3: **+200 kcal/day** above pre-pregnancy, more when training regularly.
  - Postnatal + breastfeeding: **+500 kcal/day** to support milk production.
- **Macronutrients tied to function & sources:** protein (tissue growth, muscle recovery — lean meat, eggs, legumes, dairy, tofu); carbs (primary training fuel — wholegrains, oats, sweet potato, fruit; limit refined sugar); fats (fetal brain development, milk production — oily fish 2×/week, avocado, nuts, olive oil); fibre (combats constipation — vegetables, pulses, wholegrains).
- **Key micronutrients:** folic acid, Vitamin D, iron, calcium, Vitamin C.
- **Foods to avoid & why:** raw/undercooked meat and eggs, high-mercury fish (shark, swordfish, marlin), raw shellfish, alcohol (avoid completely), caffeine above ~200 mg/day, aged/unpasteurised dairy.
- **Hydration protocol:** drink before, during, and after every session; ~2–2.5 L/day prenatal, 3 L+/day when breastfeeding.
- **Session fuelling:** pre-workout (1–2 hrs) light carb snack (banana, rice cakes, toast); post-workout (within 30–60 min) carb + protein meal (eggs and toast, rice and chicken).
