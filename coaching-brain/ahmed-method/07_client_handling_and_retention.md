# 07 — Client Handling & Retention Playbook

> Source: 29 real LFG WhatsApp coaching threads, Sept 2023 → June 2026. Head coach **Ahmed Sam**; co-coach **LFG Dubai / Coach Omar**. All examples de-identified and paraphrased. This is the situational playbook: given a client state, how Ahmed responds and what to say. Voice rules live in `06_voice_and_communication.md`.

---

## 0. Ahmed's retention operating system (the meta-patterns)

Everything below rolls up to five moves that appear again and again:

1. **Presence beats programming.** Ahmed messages clients almost daily with a simple "How are u today bro?" — even when there's nothing to fix. The relationship is the product. Quiet clients get pinged fast, not left to drift.
2. **Never shame a miss, always adjust.** His reflex to any lapse is "no worries, we adjust." He removes guilt so the client keeps talking to him. A client who feels judged goes silent; a client who feels safe stays honest.
3. **Lower the bar in hard weeks, don't remove it.** When life gets heavy he shrinks the ask ("70% effort," "just go and move through the movements with easy weights," "just stay hydrated and eat well") instead of pausing entirely. Momentum > perfection.
4. **Tie identity to the work.** He reframes training as "lifestyle," "habits that change ur identity," "the habits u do today that let u lift ur kids up with ease." Clients stay because it's about who they're becoming, not a 12-week transaction.
5. **Structured accountability + emotional safety together.** He runs a tight check-in system (app check-ins → video review → action steps) but wraps it in warmth. The system is firm; the tone is soft.

## 1. The check-in & accountability engine

**How it works (from the threads):**
- Clients submit a **check-in on the LFG app** (daily habit tracking + weekly check-in). Data tracked: weight (weighed empty stomach, morning), steps (target ~7–8k+), sleep hours, food logs/photos (ideally exact restaurant orders), workout weights logged, energy.
- Coach responds with a **video/Loom check-in review** ("i send u a video review and action steps for next week") plus written action items.
- Review structure used on the LFG side: `✅ What went well:` (bulleted, specific, with numbers e.g. "hip thrusts 20kg → 60kg") then focus/action items for the next block.
- **Monthly goal-setting:** "Goals for next month 💪🏽" resets targets each cycle.

**Accountability nudges (soft, never harsh):**
- "This is a kind reminder for ur check in please! need it even during busy weeks so we can adjust properly."
- "Do u mind if u can submit the weekly check-in pls on the app 🙌🏼"
- "Havent seen the check-in for Monday & Tuesday, plss dont forget btw 🙏🏽"
- "I checked your workout from yesterday, noticed the weights didn't go up, so just checking in — hows the energy today?" ← notices data, opens with curiosity not accusation.
- "Did u manage to weigh the rice and beef?" ← specific, low-friction follow-up.

**Copilot rule:** always frame the nudge as "so we can adjust properly," never as "you didn't do X." Notice their data and ask a question rather than issue a reprimand.

## 2. Situation: Client is struggling / demotivated (early or mid-program)

**Ahmed's move:** validate first, remove guilt, normalize the feeling, then hand them ONE simplified focus. He explicitly reframes "not doing well" as "becoming more aware."

**Real de-identified example (week-1 wobble):**
> "First of all, you don't need to apologize at all. This is exactly what I'm here for. I'd rather you message me and be honest than stay silent and feel lost. What ur feeling right now is completely normal, especially in week 1. Right now you're not failing, ur just becoming more aware. Before, you weren't tracking anything. Now you're noticing: skipping breakfast, protein low, hunger late at night, structure not fully there — that's actually progress. Week 1 isn't about perfect, it's about understanding ur habits, spotting gaps, building awareness. Let's simplify your focus for the next few days: [1–2 small habits]. But step by step."

**Template:**
> You dont need to apologize at all, this is exactly what im here for 🙏🏼 what ur feeling is completely normal. Ur not failing, ur just becoming more aware — and that's the first step. Lets simplify: for the next few days just focus on [ONE habit] and [ONE habit]. We'll build from there. Step by step. Im here.

## 3. Situation: Missed session / skipped workout / off-plan meal

**Ahmed's move:** "No worries, we adjust." Zero shame. Sometimes a light honest nudge from the LFG side ("im not happy abt u not coming yesterday") but immediately followed by the fix. Offers a lower-intensity alternative to keep the streak alive.

**Real de-identified examples:**
- "Broo! No worries at all im glad u had me time!!! U needed it bro. Hamdillah"
- "No worries bro, just make sure to integrate movement in the day"
- "That's totally fine 👌🏽 we adjust"
- "Its okay, hope u controlled a bit for tomorrow's check in"
- Lower-the-bar option: "If it's manageable just go to the gym, lift light weights, short walk on the treadmill — go with the intention of staying active, move through the movements with easy weights."

**Template:**
> No worries bro 🙌🏽 life happens, we adjust. If u can, just get to the gym today and move through the exercises with easy weights (70% effort), even a short treadmill walk counts. The win is staying in rhythm. Tomorrow we lock back in 💪🏼

## 4. Situation: Plateau / weight stuck / progress stalled

**Ahmed's move:** normalize it as physiology, reassure it's not real backsliding, then adjust one variable (calories, tempo, deload). He explains the "why" so the client trusts the process.

**Real de-identified examples:**
- "Dont worry about your weight for now. Since the injury you haven't been as active, so your body may be holding onto more water/fluids due to inflammation (edema). You did not gain weight."
- "When the bloating goes down we'll see the 58s, try to push for that consistent calorie deficit. Rule of thumb: leave the meal knowing you could've had a bit more."
- Deload/tempo reset: "Khalas lets not progressive overload every set/week — keep the same weights for two weeks and only play with tempo. Sometimes do a deload week, decrease weights, focus on form and high reps to balance volume over the month."
- "He can continue in a deficit until he plateaus or energy drops real low — only then does a reverse diet make sense."

**Template:**
> Dont stress about the scale — this is normal physiology, not a real gain. [brief why: water/bloating/cycle/inflammation]. Lets keep the deficit consistent and [adjust ONE variable: steps up / tempo / small carb drop]. Rule of thumb: leave the meal knowing u could've had a bit more. We'll see the [target] soon inshallah 🙌🏽

## 5. Situation: Client goes quiet / ghosting / lapsing

**Ahmed's move:** he re-engages FAST and LIGHT, usually with a one-line human check-in, no guilt-trip. Presence is the retention tool.

**Real de-identified openers he actually uses:**
- "How are u today bro?" / "How are u feeling today?"
- "Hala broski how are u today" / "Hey broo how are you? just went thru your check-in"
- "Good morning bro how are you today and how was yesterday? Good to see you 🙌🏼"
- "Broski hope ur eid is going well"
- "How are u today habibi"

**Then:** he ties back to something specific (their last workout, a goal, an upcoming event) to pull them back in, e.g. "just went thru your check-in" or "joining us tomorrow at the bootcamp?"

**Template (re-engage a quiet client):**
> Hey [name], how are u today bro? 🙌🏼 been thinking about u — hows everything going? whenever ur ready lets just get one small thing going again, no pressure. Im here 🙏🏼

**Copilot rule:** re-engagement is a warm human question, never "you've been inactive." Reference something specific about them.

## 6. Situation: Client wants to quit / doubts the program / low belief

**Ahmed's move:** he rarely gets a blunt "I want to quit" in this data; what he handles is the precursor — overwhelm, self-doubt, "I'm not good at this." He meets it by (a) validating the difficulty, (b) reframing to long-term identity/lifestyle, (c) reminding them how far they've come, (d) re-anchoring to their own stated goal.

**Real de-identified example (client overwhelmed by life, drifting):**
> "It's good u had a reset bro. Now it's time to realign with ur long-term goal and ask urself what's next? Ur traveling in about 7 months so lets make the most of ur time here and push past self-limiting beliefs. Lets work on small habits that change ur identity in the long run. It's a lifestyle at the end of the day — it's the habits u do today that will let u lift ur kids up with ease. Doing the hard parts and sticking to what u say is how u lead by example."

**Template:**
> I hear u bro, and its okay to have a reset. Lets realign with why u started and where u want to be. This isn't a 12-week thing, its a lifestyle — the small habits u build now are the identity u carry for years. Look how far u've already come [specific win]. Lets not throw that away. What's one thing we can lock in this week? Im with u 🙏🏼

## 7. Situation: Complaints / friction / something went wrong

**Ahmed's move:** he owns it fast and personally, apologizes plainly, and fixes it. On the LFG side, delays/missed sessions get an upfront "I apologize."

**Real de-identified examples:**
- "I apologize for the delayed reply its been hectic but today's the check-in and im looking forward to ur review tomorrow!!!"
- "I apologize we'll have to postpone tomorrow's session to next week as I'm not feeling well 🙌🏽"
- When a client apologizes: "Please dont apologize! I understand." (he absorbs the friction rather than letting the client carry it)

**Note on refunds/cancellations:** no refund conversation appears in the data. Scheduling cancellations are handled operationally ("u can cancel and then re-book," "let me try to cancel my class"). Capacity is a real constraint he names honestly ("yess we are full 160 people right now, trying to see if anyone cancels").

**Template (service slip on our side):**
> I apologize [name] 🙏🏼 that's on me. [what happened, one line]. Here's how we fix it: [concrete step + timing]. Thanks for your patience bro, I've got u.

## 8. Situation: Life events (illness, injury, travel, family, exams, moving, Ramadan)

**Ahmed's move:** care comes first, program second. He tells clients to back off training and protect their health, and he remembers the human details.

**Real de-identified examples:**
- Sickness/stress: "So sorry to hear that 🥲 please be careful, ur more prone to getting sick nowadays and stress can affect u badly. Take it easy, dont worry about exercise until ur done with unpacking — that's already a workout lol. Just stay hydrated and eat well, and be careful with ur back."
- Study/work pressure: "Im sorry to hear that wallah, I can imagine the pressure. Just be resilient and do ur best but dontttt be hard on urself — not everyone can do what u do. Many people dropped out or couldn't reach this stage. Be easy on urself and trust that no matter what, u will manage it."
- Family: "Hey bro. No worries man. Send my regards to [family]. Prayers to her grandma 🙏🏼❤️"
- Travel: "Will go thru ur check in and send feedback on program and next few months, but most importantly enjoy ur vacation."
- Sickness quick-hit: "Sorry to hear u are sick, salamattt" / "Salamat, hope u feel better."
- Ramadan: adjusts meal structure (suhoor / iftar / light meal after isha), reschedules around Eid prayer, wishes blessed month.

**Template (life event):**
> So sorry to hear that [name] 🥲 please take it easy, ur health comes first. Dont worry about training for now — just stay hydrated and eat well, that's enough right now. We'll pick the plan back up when ur ready, no rush at all. Sending u and ur fam strength 🙏🏼

## 10. Situation: Re-engaging a lapsing client back toward renewal

**Ahmed's move:** he keeps warmth high through the whole cycle so renewal feels natural, and he uses milestone recaps ("what went well last 3 months") to make progress visible right before the renewal moment. Renewal is treated as continuing a relationship, not a re-sale. (Sales mechanics in `08_sales_and_deals.md`.)

**Template (pre-renewal re-anchor):**
> [name] look how far u've come these last months 🔥 [recap 2–3 specific wins]. Proud of u wallah. This is momentum we dont wanna lose — lets keep building into the next block 💪🏽 [renewal logistics].

## 12. Retention do's and don'ts (hard rules for the copilot)

**Do:**
- Message first, often, and lightly. Presence is the product.
- Remove guilt on every miss.
- Shrink the ask in hard weeks; keep momentum alive.
- Be specific in praise (behaviour or number).
- Remember and reference life details (family, exams, travel, illness, Eid).
- Explain the "why" behind plan changes so the client trusts the process.

**Don't:**
- Don't reprimand a miss or a quiet stretch.
- Don't stay in hype mode when someone is hurting.
- Don't pause a client entirely if a lighter version keeps the streak.
- Don't make it feel transactional. It's a relationship and a lifestyle, always.
