The member
experience

This is what a runner sees. Five screens take them from never-heard-of-you to checked-in regular.

The home page

The front door. Cinematic, fast, built for phones. The three buttons send people exactly where you want them: join the next run, book a bootcamp, or enquire about private coaching. A profile icon, top-right, takes existing members to their account.

LFG Dubai · The member experience03
02 · Member experience

Booking a bootcamp

The Sunday bootcamp page is built to convert. A runner picks a date, picks a single session or a credit pack, and pays by card in a window that stays on your site. The moment the payment clears, their credits are added and their spot is booked. No DMs, no manual tracking, no chasing payment screenshots.

In-page card payment

Powered by Stripe, embedded inside your own page. Members never get bounced to a scary external link, which is exactly why more of them finish paying.

  • Single session or a 4 / 8 / 12 credit pack, your prices.
  • Credits never expire silently. Each pack carries its own validity.
  • A booking confirmation and calendar invite are emailed automatically.
  • Referral codes give both people 15% off at checkout.
Why this matters

Every booking is money in the bank before the session, and a spot you can count. You walk into Sunday knowing exactly who is coming and that they have already paid.

02 · Member experience

Their account & the leaderboard

Once someone joins, they get a personal account. It shows their credits, sessions attended, their current points, upcoming bookings and a referral code. It installs to their phone home screen like a real app. The leaderboard turns showing up into a competition.

LFG Dubai · The member experience05
02 · Member experience

Checking in at the run

You print one QR code and hold it up at the run. Runners scan it with their phone. If they have an account they are checked in instantly and earn their points. If they are brand new, the same scan walks them through a 20-second sign-up, then confirms them on the spot. The QR is smart: the same code works every Wednesday and Saturday, and the system knows which run is live.

No account? No problem

A new runner who scans gets signed up and checked in in the same flow, and lands in your member list automatically. That is how a stranger at the run becomes a contact you can nurture toward a paid bootcamp.

  • Check-in only works at the run, from 90 minutes before to a window after the start.
  • Scanning twice never double-counts.
  • Every run check-in is 50 points toward the leaderboard (bootcamp check-ins are 100).

Your admin
portal

This is your command center. Seven tabs run the entire club. Here is how to get in and what each one does.

Getting in

  1. Go to lfgdubai.com/admin.html on any phone or laptop.
  2. Sign in with the Google account we marked as owner. Only that account sees the portal.
  3. You land on Today. The tabs run down the left: Today, Overview, Bootcamp, Members, Schedule, Runs, Promo codes.
Tip

Add the admin page to your phone's home screen once. From then on it opens like an app, and you can run check-ins, mark attendance and award points from the floor.

Adjusting credits & awarding points

Two simple pop-ups, opened from any member row. Adjust credits comps or removes bootcamp credits (a rained-out session, a cash payment, a friend's first one). Award points gives leaderboard points for effort at bootcamp. Both ask for a reason and keep a record, so you always know who did what.

Good to know

Awarding points never charges anyone and never touches their credits. Points are purely for the leaderboard. Credits are what they pay for and spend on bookings. They are two separate things.

The points system

Points turn showing up into a game. The more someone trains with you, the higher they climb. It runs quietly in the background and keeps people coming back.

10points per run

Every Wednesday or Saturday run a member checks into, automatically.

20points per bootcamp

Every Sunday bootcamp they scan into, automatically.

+your bonus points

You add extra points by hand for effort, PBs or whatever you want to reward.

=the leaderboard

Everything adds up on a public board. Members see their rank and chase the next one.

The formula

Total points = (runs × 10) + (bootcamps × 20) + your bonus points.

The prize angle (keep this to yourself)

Members do not need to know there is anything at the end. Run it as bragging rights. When you are ready, reward the top of the board with a prize and watch attendance jump. The points have been quietly counting the whole time.

Everyday how-to

Mark attendance by hand (only if they didn't scan)

  1. Scanning the QR already marks people attended. Use this only for someone who didn't scan.
  2. Open Bootcamp tab, find them on the roster.
  3. Tap attended. Their 100 points land.

Award bonus points

  1. Open Members, find the person.
  2. Tap + Points.
  3. Type the number, add a note, Award.

Comp a credit

  1. Open Members, find the person.
  2. Tap Adjust, set the amount.
  3. Add a reason, Apply.

Change a run time

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Set the new day, time or location.
  3. Save. The whole site updates.

Chase a lead on WhatsApp

  1. Open Runs, scroll to leads.
  2. Tap the WhatsApp button by their name.
  3. The message is pre-written. Send.

Print your QR codes

  1. Run QR lives in Runs.
  2. Bootcamp QR lives in Bootcamp.
  3. Print once, reuse every week.

Part 06

Your weekly rhythm

You do not need to live in the admin portal. A few small habits keep the machine running and the money flowing.

Every morning · 30 seconds

Open Today. Glance at revenue, new members and which Sundays are filling slowly.

At every run

Hold up the run QR. Let new faces scan and sign up on the spot. They are now in your list forever.

Every Sunday

Open Bootcamp, let members scan the bootcamp QR, mark attendance, upload the group photo, award bonus points to anyone who earned it.

Once a week · 5 minutes

Open Runs, work the leads list. One tap each on WhatsApp turns runners into paying bootcampers. This is where the revenue is.

The one habit that pays

Working the leads list every week is the single highest-value thing you can do in here. Those runners already trust you. They just have not booked yet.
