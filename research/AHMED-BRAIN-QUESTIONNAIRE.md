# Coach Ahmed — Brain Sharpening Questionnaire

**Why this exists:** we've already built your coaching AI from your real calls, chats, and
methods. It sounds like you and thinks like you. This fills the few gaps it can't get from
the data: your actual numbers and how you personally make the calls. Every answer here
makes the assistant draft plans and replies closer to exactly what you'd do.

**How to answer:** voice-note anything you want, we'll transcribe it. Type the short ones.
Skip anything that doesn't apply or you'd rather not share. There are no wrong answers, and
"it depends on X" is a great answer because that's the rule we're actually after. The
⭐ questions are the highest-impact, do those first if you're short on time.

---

## 1. Your numbers & starting defaults ⭐ (how you personally set things)

1. ⭐ New fat-loss client: how do you set their starting calories? (Formula, bodyweight
   multiplier, or your gut range?) Same question for muscle-gain and maintenance.
2. ⭐ Your default protein target? (g per kg, or per lb, or a flat number.)
3. Default daily step target for a new client, and how you progress it.
4. ⭐ Default training frequency you prescribe per package (e.g. 3 days for Basic, 4–5 for
   premium)? What's your go-to split?
5. How long before you change a plan if the scale isn't moving? (e.g. 2 weeks flat → act.)
6. Your deload / diet-break cadence: every how many weeks, and how do you decide?
7. How much of a weekly weight change do you consider "on track" vs "too fast/slow"?
8. Which few metrics do you actually look at first on a check-in before anything else?

## 2. How you decide (your real rules) ⭐

9. ⭐ Scale hasn't moved in 2 weeks. Walk me through what you check and what you change,
   in order. (This is the single most useful answer for the whole brain.)
10. Client had a bad/low-energy week. What do you look at before you touch the program?
11. When do you push a struggling client harder vs. pull back and lower the bar?
12. What makes you change someone's whole program vs. just tweak one thing?
13. Your red flags: what in a check-in makes you stop and refer out to a doctor/physio/
    dietitian/therapist instead of coaching through it?
14. When do you decide a client isn't a fit and you'd rather not renew them?

## 3. Programming specifics

15. Your go-to exercises you put in almost every program, and any you avoid/hate.
16. Default equipment assumption (full gym, home, minimal)? How do you adjust when limited?
17. How you program cardio/conditioning alongside lifting.
18. HYROX / running / hybrid clients: what changes in your approach vs a physique client?
19. Beginner vs intermediate vs advanced: what actually changes in how you program?

## 4. Nutrition specifics

20. ⭐ Your 3M framework (Meals, Macros, Market) in practice: how do you actually walk a
    new client through it in week one?
21. Go-to meals / templates you recommend for UAE clients (local foods, eating out,
    delivery). Anything you steer people toward or away from.
22. How you handle refeeds, cheat meals, weekends, and social events.
23. ⭐ Ramadan: how you adjust training and nutrition for fasting clients.
24. Supplements you actually recommend (by role, not brand), and ones you tell people to
    skip/save money on.
25. Women's cycle: how you coach training and food around the phases in practice.

## 5. Hard client situations — your actual scripts ⭐

For each, tell me roughly what you'd send. The brain drafts these; your real wording makes
them sound like you and not a generic coach.

26. ⭐ A client goes quiet for 2 weeks (no check-in, no reply). What do you send?
27. A client wants to quit / cancel mid-program. Your message?
28. ⭐ Renewal time: how and when do you bring up continuing, and what do you say?
29. A client is frustrated they're "not seeing results." Your response?
30. A client asks for a refund. How do you handle it (message + policy)?
31. Someone breaks a hard week (travel, illness, family emergency). Your holding message?
32. A client crushes a milestone (PB, goal weight, first pull-up). Your celebration message?

## 6. Voice corrections & additions

33. Read a couple of AI-drafted messages we'll send you: what's off, what you'd change,
    what you'd never say. (We'll attach these; your corrections train the voice directly.)
34. Any phrases, sayings, or emojis you use a lot that we might have missed.
35. Anything the assistant should NEVER say to a client (words, tone, topics).

## 7. Your story & positioning (for sales/authority)

36. Short version of your origin: how you got into coaching and why. (Voice-note is perfect.)
37. Your credentials/certs and anything that gives you authority in the client's eyes.
38. Who is your ideal client, and who are you genuinely NOT the right coach for?
39. What do clients say you do differently from other coaches? Your real edge, in your words.
40. Any proof you're proud of (transformations, results, retention) that we can reference
    without breaking anyone's privacy.

## 8. "The brain would get this wrong" — corrections ⭐

41. ⭐ Think of 2–3 recent real clients. For each: the situation, and the exact call you
    made and why. Real cases teach the brain more than any rule.
42. Is there anything the current AI assistant gets wrong about how you coach, that you
    want fixed? Tell us straight.

## 9. Anything else

43. Anything we didn't ask that matters to how you coach or run LFG? Dump it here.

---

*Answer in whatever order suits you. Voice notes for the long ones are ideal. The more
specific and real the examples, the sharper your assistant gets.*
