What Is LFG Dubai

WHAT IS LFG DUBAI

LFG Dubai is a Dubai-born running club and fitness community where community meets performance. We bring people together through weekly runs, group training, events, and structured programs designed to take you from zero to competition. No matter your level, you will be supported, challenged, and pushed to grow inside a community that shows up and puts in the work. Powered by our partnership with PUMA Middle East and backed by certified NASM coaching, we are here to help you build your strongest body and mindset.

LFG Dubai is also a members platform: create a free account to reserve your spot at our Sunday Bootcamp, sign up for weekly runs, check in at sessions with a QR code, track your attendance and points, and manage your bookings and credits. You can sign in securely with your Google account or an email login link.
